00Read this first

Your strongest idea is the one your website never says.

You told us that lasting enterprises begin with a leader who has clarity of purpose. That belief is the reason TOGC is different from every other consultancy in Lagos. It does not appear anywhere on your homepage.

What's wrong
The site sells a category, not a conviction.

The hero calls TOGC a “growth and transformation strategic partner”. That could be any firm. The person-first insight, the thing only you say, is absent. Meanwhile a print-and-signage portfolio sits high on the homepage, so visitors conclude you are a branding vendor.

What's missing
Proof, prices, dates, and you.

No named clients, no results, no founder. The Academy lists nine programmes with no dates, fees or outcomes, four of which are not running. Someone ready to buy cannot find out what it costs or when it starts.

What to do
Two focus areas, two ways in, one first step.

Personal Growth and Business Growth are what you do. Private consulting and the Growth Academy are how you deliver it. “Growth Training” stops being a third service. Every page ends with one action: book a Discovery Session.

Why it matters
Confused visitors don't enquire, they leave.

The homepage asks a visitor to weigh three services, four button labels and nine programmes with no cue about which is theirs. The contact form then requires a company name, which turns away the personal-growth client who is your core audience.

Eight findings, in order of cost

Each line gives the finding and the fix. Expand for the evidence from the live site.

01Your core belief is missing from the two places that matter The hero and the About statement describe a category. Neither mentions purpose, clarity or identity. See the evidence +
On the site now

Hero: “We are your growth and transformation strategic partner.”

About: “We are an integrated growth and transformation solutions company empowering individuals, businesses, and organizations to achieve sustainable growth.”

Your words to us: the problem is not capacity, it is identity. People build businesses to survive rather than to build institutions.

The gap between those is the whole opportunity. Your framing is specific, arguable and memorable. The site's framing is none of those. “Individuals, businesses, and organizations” also names the entire market, so the person-first emphasis is swallowed by the business words around it.

Fix

A new hero built on the person-first belief, plus a “Why we start with the person” block replacing the About paragraph. Copy in section 06.

02Three services sit side by side, but one of them is a delivery method Personal Growth, Business Growth and Growth Training are presented as equals. The third is how you deliver the first two. See the evidence +

A visitor reading the three tiles cannot tell whether Growth Training is a different service, a cheaper version, or the same thing in a classroom. It is the last of these, and presenting it as a peer makes all three harder to understand.

It also creates the exclusivity problem you raised. A high-net-worth individual who sees “training” beside the premium offer reads it as a signal that this is not for people like them.

Fix

Two focus areas (Personal, Business), two routes (private consulting, Growth Academy). The Academy keeps its navigation slot and gains a strong section of its own, but leaves the service row.

03Four calls to action, no routing, and a form that blocks your core client The contact form requires a company name, which excludes personal-growth clients entirely. See the evidence +
On the site now

Four labels lead to a contact route: “Contact”, “Let's get started”, “Book a Discovery Call”, and the navigation's “Let's Collaborate”.

The form requires name, company name, how can we help, and service. The service list offers Strategy, Business Transformation, Personal Branding, Corporate Branding, Marketing, Growth & Learning and General Inquiries.

No Personal Mastery. No Academy programme. “Business Transformation” has no page. No confirmation message appears after submitting.

An executive seeking private clarity work has no company to enter and no matching option to pick. This is the most expensive single defect on the site, because it fails the visitor who has already decided to act.

Fix

One routing form, three questions, optional organisation field, and a confirmation message that says when you will reply. Copy in section 06.

03The fix

Two focus areas. Two ways in. One first step.

The positioning, in one sentence

TOGC International helps people discover who they are and why they exist, then builds the enterprises that express it, so that leaders and businesses across Africa are built to last, not just to survive.

Drawn from your own account. Everything in section 06 is written to support this sentence.

The holding-company story is real but not yet true on all three legs. Publishing it early invites a question you cannot answer.

Why the person comes first, and why both routes get equal weight The reasoning behind the structure, including your own economics. Expand +
The insight

Africa's enterprise gap is not capacity, it is identity. Most businesses were started to survive, by founders never helped to ask why they exist. Other consultancies ask “what do you want to build?” before “who are you?”. TOGC reverses that order, and the reversal is the product.

The two engines

You described consulting as the stronger earner per client, and the Academy as the stronger reach: more people served for less effort per person. Both belong on the homepage with equal weight, because one funds the work and the other spreads it. Neither should be described as the lesser option.

The step names reuse language already on your site (Identity Alignment, Purpose Architecture, Capability Acceleration). Confirm they match how you actually work.

The Academy's three formats, for context

You run programmes at three depths. The full programme is the complete journey from discovery through development. The abridged workshop is a first-level intervention on the most common issues. The modular version splits the full programme into parts people can take one cohort at a time.

What launches now is the abridged tier. That is why SMC comes off the site: it is the modular form of PLAY, not a different subject, and it is a format you are not running yet. Worth stating internally, so the hidden list reads as sequencing rather than retreat.

Because Academy programmes can be linked directly from campaigns and the brochure, they do not need homepage prominence to sell. That reinforces keeping the homepage focused.

Writing for public figures without business jargon A rule that protects your most exclusive clients. Expand +

You made this point precisely. Celebrities, musicians and actors are not in business the way a founder is. Offering them business design, process engineering and growth strategy misreads what they came for. What they want is to discover themselves, get clear on who they are, and integrate the many expressions of their work into one coherent voice.

The rule: on Personal Growth, Personal Mastery and Personal Branding, no operational business vocabulary. No process, systems, optimisation, scalability or profitability. Those words belong on Business Growth. The personal pages speak in the language of identity, direction, presence and legacy.

This is also why Personal Mastery must not open with a personal-branding sentence, as it does today. The private client and the public client need different first lines.

05Why it works

The behavioural reasoning, if you want it

Visitors are not lazy, they are overloaded. The homepage asks them to weigh three services, four button labels and nine programmes with no signal about which is theirs. When choice rises and guidance does not, the easiest decision is to leave.

Primary barrier: capability — visitors cannot tell what is on offer or which part is for them. Secondary: trust — a premium, personal purchase is being asked for without proof.
